Why I stopped reviewing AI drafts in a chat window

For most of this year I reviewed every AI draft the same way: read it in one window, type my notes into another, and wait. It worked, in the sense that posts got published. It did not work in the sense that I enjoyed any of it.

What I tried first

My first fix was to paste the draft into a document, mark it up with comments, and paste the comments back into the chat. The agent was good at reading them. I was bad at keeping the two copies in step.

Then I tried asking for one paragraph at a time. That kept the chat short, but it meant I never read the post as a whole until the end, which is exactly when a structural problem is most expensive to fix.

What changed

Editing this blockParagraph ▾BIDelete blockCmd-Enter or Esc to finish

Now I read the draft on the page itself, as a reader would, and leave my notes and edits right where they belong. The agent picks them up from there. I still type, but I type on the post, not about it.

The part I did not expect is that I write more of each post myself now, because adding a paragraph no longer costs me a round trip.
